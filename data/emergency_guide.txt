# OFFICIAL EMERGENCY RESPONSE MANUAL

## SECTION 1: FIRE EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
1. IMMEDIATE ACTIONS UPON DISCOVERING A FIRE:
   - Stay calm and immediately sound the nearest fire alarm call point.
   - Call local emergency services (911 or local emergency number) immediately.
   - If the fire is small and contained (e.g., in a waste basket) and you are trained, you may attempt to extinguish it using a suitable fire extinguisher. Never let the fire come between you and the exit.
   - Remember the PASS method for fire extinguishers: Pull the pin, Aim low at the base of the fire, Squeeze the lever, Sweep from side to side.

2. EVACUATION PROTOCOL:
   - Evacuate the building immediately using marked emergency exits and stairwells.
   - DO NOT use elevators under any circumstances during a fire.
   - Stay low to the ground if smoke is present. Smoke and hot toxic gases rise; cleaner air is near the floor.
   - Touch doors with the back of your hand before opening. If a door is hot, DO NOT open it. Use an alternate escape route.
   - Close doors behind you as you evacuate to contain the spread of fire and smoke.
   - Proceed directly to the designated Emergency Assembly Area outside the building.

3. IF TRAPPED INSIDE A ROOM:
   - Close all doors between you and the fire.
   - Seal cracks around doors and air vents using wet towels, clothing, or tape to prevent smoke entry.
   - Signal for help at a window by waving a bright cloth or flashlight. Call emergency services to report your exact location.


## SECTION 2: EARTHQUAKE SAFETY PROTOCOLS
1. INDOOR SAFETY (DROP, COVER, AND HOLD ON):
   - DROP down onto your hands and knees. This position protects you from being knocked down and allows you to move if necessary.
   - COVER your head and neck under a sturdy table or desk. If no shelter is nearby, crawl next to an interior wall away from windows, heavy furniture, or hanging objects.
   - HOLD ON to your shelter until shaking stops. Be prepared to move with your shelter if it shifts.
   - DO NOT run outside while shaking is occurring. Falling debris, exterior walls, and glass pose extreme danger.
   - Stay clear of windows, glass, exterior doors, walls, and heavy light fixtures.

2. OUTDOOR SAFETY:
   - Move to an open area away from buildings, streetlights, utility wires, and trees.
   - Drop to the ground and cover your head until shaking ceases.

3. IN A VEHICLE:
   - Pull over safely to the side of the road, away from overpasses, bridges, power lines, and tall buildings.
   - Keep seatbelts fastened and remain inside the vehicle until shaking stops.

4. AFTER THE SHAKING STOPS:
   - Expect aftershocks. Be prepared to Drop, Cover, and Hold On again.
   - Check yourself and others for injuries. Apply basic first aid if trained.
   - Inspect utilities: If you smell gas, turn off the main gas valve if safe to do so, open windows, and evacuate immediately. DO NOT turn electrical switches on or off or use open flames.


## SECTION 3: FLOOD & FLASH FLOOD RESPONSE
1. PRE-FLOOD & WARNING STAGE:
   - Monitor local weather alerts and emergency broadcasting stations.
   - Move critical valuables, documentation, and electronics to upper floors.
   - Charge cell phones and portable power banks.

2. DURING A FLOOD:
   - Evacuate immediately if instructed by emergency officials or if floodwaters begin to rise around your building.
   - Move to higher ground immediately. Avoid low-lying areas, canyons, and storm drains.
   - TURN AROUND, DON'T DROWN: Never walk, swim, or drive through floodwaters. As little as 6 inches of moving water can knock you down, and 12 inches can sweep away a car.
   - If trapped in a building by rising water, move to the top floor or roof. DO NOT climb into a closed attic where you might become trapped by rising water. Signal for rescue.

3. POST-FLOOD SAFETY:
   - Avoid contact with floodwater—it may be contaminated with sewage, dangerous chemicals, or charged by downed power lines.
   - Do not enter damaged buildings until local authorities declare them structurally safe.


## SECTION 4: HAZARDOUS MATERIAL & TORNADO/STORM EMERGENCY
1. SHELTER-IN-PLACE (HAZMAT OR CHEMICAL RELEASE):
   - Move indoors immediately. Close all exterior doors and windows.
   - Turn off all heating, ventilation, and air conditioning (HVAC) systems and fans to prevent drawing outside air indoors.
   - Go to an interior room above ground level with few or no windows.
   - Seal doors, windows, and vents with plastic sheeting and duct tape if instructed by authorities.
   - Stay tuned to official emergency news channels for clearance.

2. TORNADO & SEVERE WIND PROTOCOL:
   - Go to the lowest level of the building (basement or storm shelter).
   - If no basement is available, go to a small interior room on the lowest floor (bathroom, closet, interior hallway) away from exterior walls and windows.
   - Protect your body with a mattress, heavy blankets, or helmets. Cover your head with your arms.
