# EMERGENCY FIRST AID & TRIAGE PROTOCOLS

## SECTION 1: LIFE-SAVING FIRST AID PROCEDURES

1. CARDIOPULMONARY RESUSCITATION (CPR):
   - Assess scene safety: Ensure the area is safe for both you and the victim.
   - Check responsiveness: Tap shoulders and shout "Are you okay?".
   - Call 911 / Emergency Services immediately or direct someone specific to call and get an Automated External Defibrillator (AED).
   - Check breathing: Look for normal breathing for 5 to 10 seconds.
   - CPR (Hands-Only CPR for untrained lay responders):
     - Place heel of one hand in center of victim's chest, place other hand on top, interlocking fingers.
     - Push hard and fast in center of chest at a rate of 100 to 120 compressions per minute (to beat of "Stayin' Alive").
     - Allow chest to recoil completely between compressions.
     - Continue until emergency medical personnel arrive or an AED is ready.

2. SEVERE BLEEDING (HEMORRHAGE) CONTROL:
   - Call emergency medical services immediately.
   - Wear protective gloves if available.
   - Apply DIRECT PRESSURE: Place a clean cloth, sterile gauze, or gloved hand directly over the wound and apply firm, continuous pressure.
   - Do NOT remove soaked dressings; apply additional cloths over top and maintain pressure.
   - If severe arterial bleeding on a limb continues and direct pressure fails, apply a commercial tourniquet 2-3 inches above the wound (never over a joint), tighten until bleeding stops, and note exact time of application for emergency personnel.

3. CHOKING (ADULT / CHILD OVER 1 YEAR):
   - Ask "Are you choking?". If person cannot speak, cough strongly, or breathe, act immediately.
   - Perform Heimlich Maneuver (Abdominal Thrusts):
     - Stand behind the person, wrap arms around their waist.
     - Make a fist with one hand, place thumb side against abdomen slightly above navel and well below breastbone.
     - Grasp fist with other hand and press into abdomen with quick upward thrusts.
     - Repeat thrusts until object is expelled or person becomes unconscious.
     - If victim becomes unconscious, lower to floor, call 911, and begin CPR compressions. Check mouth for visible object before giving breaths.

4. BURNS (THERMAL & CHEMICAL):
   - Thermal Burns: Cool burn immediately under cool running water for at least 10-20 minutes. Do NOT use ice, ice water, butter, or oil. Cover loosely with sterile non-stick bandage or clean cloth. Do not break blisters.
   - Chemical Burns: Flush skin or eyes continuously with clean running water for at least 20 minutes while removing contaminated clothing. Call emergency services.

5. HEAT STROKE (MEDICAL EMERGENCY):
   - Symptoms: High body temperature (>103°F/39.4°C), hot/red/dry skin, rapid strong pulse, confusion, dizziness, loss of consciousness.
   - Actions: Call 911 immediately. Move person to a cool, shaded area. Cool rapidly using ice packs under armpits/groin, wet towels, or cold water immersion. Do NOT give fluids if person is confused or unconscious.
