DOCUMENT TITLE: Fire Emergency Response & Extinguisher Guidance
DOCUMENT ID: DOC-FIRE-2026-v1

1. FIRE SAFETY PROCEDURES:
- In case of a fire, immediately activate the nearest fire alarm pull station.
- Call local emergency services (911 or local emergency dispatcher).
- If the fire is small and contained, use a fire extinguisher following the PASS rule:
  * Pull the pin.
  * Aim at the base of the fire.
  * Squeeze the handle.
  * Sweep from side to side.
- For kitchen grease fires: NEVER pour water on a grease fire! Turn off the stove burner if safe, and cover the pan with a metal lid or baking soda to smother the flames.

2. EVACUATION PROTOCOL:
- Leave the building immediately using stairs. DO NOT use elevators during a fire emergency.
- If smoke is present, stay low to the floor where the air is cooler and cleaner.
- Touch door handles before opening. If a door is hot, do NOT open it—use an alternate exit route.
- Close doors behind you as you leave to isolate fire and smoke.
- Assemble at the designated outdoor assembly point and stay together until counted by safety personnel.
