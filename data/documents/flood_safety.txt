DOCUMENT TITLE: Flood & Flash Flood Safety Guide
DOCUMENT ID: DOC-FLOOD-2026-v1

1. FLOOD WARNING & ACTION PLAN:
- Monitor local emergency broadcasts and NOAA weather radio alerts.
- Move critical supplies, food, medications, and valuable documents to higher ground or upper floors.
- Turn off main electricity and gas lines if instructed by authorities and if safe to do so.

2. DURING A FLOOD EMERGENCY:
- Evacuate immediately if ordered by local emergency officials.
- TURN AROUND, DON'T DROWN: Never drive, walk, or swim through floodwaters. 6 inches of fast-moving water can knock an adult down; 12 inches can float a car.
- Avoid contact with floodwater—it can be contaminated with sewage, chemicals, or charged by submerged power lines.
- If trapped in a building, move to the top floor or roof. DO NOT climb into a closed attic space where you could become trapped by rising water.

3. RE-ENTRY SAFETY:
- Return home only when emergency authorities announce it is safe.
- Do not enter any building surrounded by floodwaters until certified structurally sound.
