DOCUMENT TITLE: Earthquake Safety & Shelter Protocols
DOCUMENT ID: DOC-EQ-2026-v1

1. INDOOR EARTHQUAKE SAFETY (DROP, COVER, AND HOLD ON):
- DROP down onto your hands and knees immediately to prevent being knocked down.
- COVER your head and neck under a sturdy table, desk, or against an interior wall away from windows, glass, mirrors, and tall unsecured furniture.
- HOLD ON to your shelter until the shaking stops completely. Be prepared to move with your shelter if it shifts.
- DO NOT run outside while shaking is occurring. Falling glass, brickwork, and debris cause the majority of injuries.

2. OUTDOOR & VEHICLE EARTHQUAKE SAFETY:
- If outdoors: Move to a clear area away from buildings, utility wires, streetlights, and overpasses. Drop to the ground.
- If driving: Pull safely to the side of the road away from overpasses, power lines, and tall structures. Stay inside the vehicle with seatbelts fastened until shaking stops.

3. POST-EARTHQUAKE CHECKLIST:
- Check for injuries and provide first aid.
- Inspect utility lines for gas leaks. If you smell gas, shut off the main gas valve if safe, open windows, and evacuate immediately. Do NOT flip electrical switches.
- Expect aftershocks—be ready to Drop, Cover, and Hold On again.
