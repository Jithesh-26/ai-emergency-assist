DOCUMENT TITLE: General Emergency Preparedness & Go-Bag Essentials
DOCUMENT ID: DOC-PREP-2026-v1

1. EMERGENCY GO-BAG ESSENTIALS:
- Water: 1 gallon per person per day for at least 3 days.
- Non-perishable food: Minimum 3-day supply of canned or dried food.
- First Aid Kit: Bandages, antiseptic wipes, tourniquet, prescription medications, pain relievers.
- Communication & Power: Battery-powered or hand-crank radio, flashlight, extra batteries, solar phone charger.
- Multi-tool, emergency whistle (to signal for help), dust masks, duct tape, and plastic sheeting.
- Important documents: Passports, IDs, insurance policies, medical records in a waterproof container.

2. FAMILY COMMUNICATION PLAN:
- Designate an out-of-town contact person everyone can call if local phone lines are jammed.
- Establish two emergency meeting places: one right outside your home and one outside your neighborhood.
- Keep paper copies of emergency phone numbers in your go-bag and wallet.
